Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

JZ Oura Dashboard ("the app") is a personal project operated by JZ ("I", "me") for my own use. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data accessed

With my authorisation through Oura's OAuth2 flow, the app reads data from my own Oura account via the Oura API, within the scopes I select. These may include personal information, daily sleep, readiness and activity summaries, heart rate, workouts, sessions, SpO2, stress, tags and related health metrics.

How data is used

Data is used only to display and analyse my own health and wellness information. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

Storage and security

Data and OAuth tokens are stored only on devices or accounts that I control, and I take reasonable steps to keep them secure. Access tokens can be revoked at any time from my Oura account settings.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, or share Oura data with any third party, and the app does not transfer data to others.

Other users

The app is not offered to the public and is not intended for use by anyone other than me. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any other person is invited to connect their account.

Retention and deletion

I may delete stored data at any time. Revoking the app's access in Oura stops further data collection.

Changes

I may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
